TITLE
2. Coordinates and reference geometry

BULLETS
- CMS axes: +x toward LHC centre; +y up; +z along the counter-clockwise beam
- Cavern frame: x=CMS x, y=−CMS z, z=CMS y
- Detector dimensions/axes: CMS detector paper
- Cavern and shaft: LHC Design Report Vol. 2 §10.3
- Beam level ≈90 m; CERN outreach commonly says 100 m underground
- North/east: CERN GIS/MapCERN; current compass is an approximate P5→P1 bearing
  - Hall, shaft, ground and detector wireframes are not survey-grade

VISUAL
Interpretive display geometry
