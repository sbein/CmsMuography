SAMPLING NOTE
The default reverse-cms-volume mode draws a point uniformly in the finite CMS
cylinder volume. EXPACS/PARMA supplies energy and zenith; azimuth is uniform.
The resulting line is intersected with the cylinder and weighted by the inverse
of its chord length. This removes the length bias from volume-point sampling and
recovers the crossing-line measure up to the common normalization. It is not a
uniform draw of impact parameter on one fixed detector face.
