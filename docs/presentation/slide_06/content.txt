TITLE
5. Observed + expected counts → Li–Ma evidence

BULLETS
- Nobs: observed voxel crossings
- Nbkg: corrected solid-ground expectation
- Li–Ma: Noff = Nbkg/α; default α = 0.05
- Outputs: signed Z, positive cavity excess, Nobs/Nbkg, log transmission
- Render 3D thresholds plus XY/XZ slice stacks
  - Interpretation must include null-model, efficiency and geometry systematics
